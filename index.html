Metal Roof Repair

Existing metal roof leaking?

I see a lot of metal roofs that were done poorly. If yours is leaking, call or email — I'll fix it properly, with the right boots and flashings, not more silicone.

  • Missing or failed pipe boots on vent pipes and sewer stacks
  • Chimneys sealed with silicone instead of proper flashing
  • Gaps filled with caulking that has cracked or let go
  • Leaks you can't trace — I'll find where the water is getting in

Metal only. I don't install asphalt shingles or flat roofs (tar, torch-on or rubber). If that's what you need, a contractor who specializes in it will serve you better.

Understanding gauge

Lower number = thicker steel. A thicker panel gives better dent resistance and a longer service life, especially against hail and Alberta weather. Tap a gauge to see where it fits.

24 gauge — premium

Best for standing seam, high-end homes, commercial buildings, and roofs over open purlins.

The thickest of the three. It is the industry standard for standing seam and engineered commercial roofs, spans open framing better, and gives the most dent resistance against hail, heavy snow and wind.

Trade-off: Costs more than 26 gauge, is heavier and harder to work, and comes in fewer colours. More steel than most houses and outbuildings need.

Profiles: All six — 9-36, 7/8" Corrugated, 12-36, Diamond Rib, Standing Seam, Snaplock.

26 gauge — most popular for homes

The usual choice for houses and larger barns.

Noticeably stiffer than 29 gauge, so it holds up better to Alberta hail, keeps panels looking straighter, and handles snow loads well. It is the balance of strength and price most homeowners pick, and it comes in every stock colour.

Trade-off: Costs a bit more than 29 gauge.

Profiles: All six — 9-36, 7/8" Corrugated, 12-36, Diamond Rib, Standing Seam, Snaplock.

29 gauge — standard

Most common on outbuildings: sheds, garages, shops, smaller barns, and wall or liner panels.

The most economical option and plenty for buildings that are not your home. Lightweight and quick to install.

Trade-off: Dents more easily in hail and shows waviness in the panels sooner. Not the pick for a house where looks and longevity matter.

Profiles: 9-36 only.

Common questions

Will snow slide off a metal roof?

Yes — metal sheds snow much faster than shingles. Over doors, walkways and decks I recommend snow guards so it comes off in a controlled way. We will go over where they make sense on your house.

Is it loud when it rains?

Over a solid roof deck with underlayment, most people hear very little difference from shingles. The noisy reputation comes mostly from tin on open framing, like an old shed.

Can you go over my old shingles?

Sometimes. It depends on how many layers are on, the condition of the deck and what the job needs. I check on site and tell you straight whether a tear-off is the better call.

How long does a metal roof last?

Decades longer than asphalt. The paint and panel warranty depends on the product and gauge you choose. I will show you the manufacturer's warranty for your panel with your quote.

What does a metal roof cost?

More up front than shingles, usually less over the life of the house since you are not reroofing every 15–20 years. Every roof is different, so I measure and give you a real number for free.
